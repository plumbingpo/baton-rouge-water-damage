Every water damage restoration job starts with extraction, and no other early step matters more. Quick removal of standing water means less harm to your flooring, your belongings, the building's systems and the structure itself. Left standing, water keeps moving deeper by the hour, wicking into drywall, soaking carpet pad, seeping under flooring and saturating the framing. Baton Rouge Restoration provides emergency water extraction throughout Baton Rouge using industrial-grade truck-mounted and portable equipment capable of removing hundreds of gallons per minute.

Truck-mounted units rank with the strongest water removal systems made, and their suction lifts water out of hard floors, carpet and structural materials much better than anything you can rent or buy. For areas truck-mounted hoses cannot reach, we deploy powerful portable extractors, submersible pumps and specialty tools designed for specific situations, including water trapped in wall cavities, between floor layers and in confined spaces.

There is more to a professional extraction than getting rid of the water in plain sight. Our IICRC-certified technicians use moisture detection equipment to identify water that has migrated into hidden areas: behind baseboards, inside wall cavities, under cabinets and beneath flooring materials. Specialty extraction tools remove as much moisture as possible from these hidden areas before the drying phase begins, which significantly reduces the time and equipment needed for structural drying and lowers the risk of secondary mold growth.

How to Prevent Water Extraction Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Shelving in low areas keeps belongings out of the water if the floor floods
  • A sump pump that has not been checked may fail in a storm, so test it ahead of wet weather
  • Leak-sensing shutoff valves can cut off the water on their own when something fails
  • Put together a simple kit with a bucket, some old towels and the phone number of your restoration company
  • Program (228) 200-3362 into your phone so you can call immediately in an emergency
  • Find the main shutoff valve for the house and put a clear label on it

What a Professional Brings to Water Extraction

Both the harm done to your home and the restoration bill depend directly on quick, thorough extraction. Truck-mounted extractors move water at hundreds of times the rate of consumer tools, and a job that could cost you a full day with a shop vac is done by our machines in minutes. Speed matters for more than comfort: every extra hour of water sitting against building materials can mean thousands of dollars more in damage.

Our extraction also goes after moisture that home equipment can neither find nor get to. Water travels by capillary action into carpet pad, wicks up drywall, runs along electrical conduits and plumbing lines, and gets between flooring layers. Our specialty extraction tools and techniques target these hidden moisture deposits, removing significantly more water than surface extraction alone. Being this thorough shortens drying, cuts equipment costs and, above all, helps stop trapped moisture from turning into hidden mold.

When to Call for Water Extraction in Baton Rouge

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Laminate or wood flooring that feels spongy and pushes up water at the seams
  • Water seeping out from under baseboards or cabinets
  • A utility area, mechanical room or crawl space with water in it
  • Flooded window wells or stairwells letting water into the building
  • Any room where water has collected on the floor
  • Rising water in the basement because the sump pump stopped working
  • Water flowing or dripping from ceilings, walls or fixtures
